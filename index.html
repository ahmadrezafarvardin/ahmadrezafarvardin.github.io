Ahmadreza Farvardin

Mathematics & Computer Science, University of Tehran

I'm a Mathematics and Computer Science student at the University of Tehran, working on computer vision, multimodal LLMs, and agents.

My work so far is in visual perception. I built a pipeline that measures river surface velocity from video by combining RAFT optical flow with classical PIV, and a handwritten math-recognition system that took 1st place in the Machine Learning course competition. I also implement models from their original papers in PyTorch, including the Transformer, YOLOv3, U-Net and ResNet-18.

Seeking Master's or research assistant positions in machine learning and computer vision, starting Spring 2027, Fall 2027, or Spring 2028.
