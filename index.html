I'm an undergraduate studying math with a CS minor, ranked 2nd of 40 in my major. Most of what I build is computer vision: finding objects in images, segmenting them, and measuring how things move.

In 2024 I worked on estimating river surface velocity from ordinary video, combining RAFT optical flow with classical PIV. Since then I've been rebuilding well-known models from their papers in plain PyTorch, with no pretrained weights and no library shortcuts like nn.Transformer: a Transformer, YOLOv3, U-Net and ResNet-18.
