Ahmadreza Farvardin

Mathematics & Computer Science, University of Tehran

I'm a Mathematics and Computer Science student at the University of Tehran, working on computer vision, multimodal LLMs, and agents.

Most recently I built AlphaEvolve-VLM, an LLM-driven program-search system that rewrites a 2B vision-language model's inference code for chart question answering. A strict holdout audit showed that its gain came from gaming the benchmark. Before that I worked on visual perception: measuring river surface velocity from video with RAFT optical flow and classical PIV, and a handwritten math-recognition system that took 1st place in the Machine Learning course competition.

Seeking Master's or research assistant positions in machine learning and computer vision, starting Spring 2027, Fall 2027, or Spring 2028.
